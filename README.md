## 📝 About This Repository

- `Lecture-Notes/` holds lecture notes that follow the textbook's chapter numbers (`Ch01`-`Ch11`).
- `Coursework/` holds explanations of the 12 homework assignments made up of textbook exercises; the submitted answer files are not included.
- Every lecture note and assignment explanation comes as an English (`.md`) and Korean (`.ko.md`) pair.

> **🤖 AI Agent Usage**
> [Claude Code](https://claude.ai/download) was used to organize and translate the notes and assignment explanations and to restructure the repository.

### Course Overview

A course taught in English on the discrete mathematics that underlies computer science, from the logic of compound and quantified statements, methods of proof, sequences and mathematical induction, sets, functions, and relations to counting and probability, graphs and trees, and the analysis of algorithm efficiency, following Susanna S. Epp's textbook. The instructor, schedule, assessment, course policies, and progress are in the [course information document](Course-Information.md).
